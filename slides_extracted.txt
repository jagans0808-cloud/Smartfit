Wired connections limit wearable mobility, while real-time integrated exercise feedback is often unavailable. | OUR SOLUTION | SmartFit |  integrates physiological, motion, joint-angle and foot-pressure sensing into a wireless 2-ESP32 wearable system, providing real-time exercise analysis, feedback and centralized web-based monitoring. | PROBLEM SOLUTION | Synchronized Acquisition:  | Multiple sensor signals are time-aligned and collected through distributed ESP32 nodes. | Feature Extraction:  | Raw signals are converted into joint angle, repetition count, motion features and pressure patterns. | Movement Classification:  | Extracted features are processed by a Random Forest model to classify predefined exercises. |  Performance Output:  | The system generates exercise status, repetition count and physiological readings for user monitoring. | INNOVATION & UNIQUENESS  | Full-Body Monitoring | : Sensors monitor chest, arm, knee and foot movements in one system. | Wireless Wearable:  | 2 ESP32 + ESP-NOW provide cable-free sensor communication. | Smart Feedback | : AI converts sensor data into exercise status and repetition feedback. | Multi-Use Design:  | Suitable for gyms, sports, rehabilitation and home fitness. | Real-Time Multi-Parameter View | : Displays 5+ fitness parameters simultaneously on one dashboard for faster training decisions.
